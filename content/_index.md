<p>
  We are pleased to announce the 2027 <i>Language Learning Cognitive Neursocience Series</i> Roundtable Conference entitled "Ecological validity in the cognitive neuroscience of language learning and processing: Promising approaches and future directions” to be held May 13-14 at Pennsylvania State University. 
  <br><br>
  This conference brings together linguists, psychologists, and cognitive neuroscientists who share an interest in the ecological validity of research on the cognitive neuroscience of language, specifically as related to adult second/additional language learning and processing. In addition to 8 keynote speakers, the roundtable conference will include talks and poster presentation sessions. The keynote speakers are (in alphabetical order): 
  <br>
    <ul>
      <li>Dr. Esti Blanco-Elorrieta (New York University)</li>
      <li>Dr. Jonathan Brennan (University of Michigan)</li>
      <li>Dr. Suzanne Dikker (New York University; Ghent University; online talk)</li>
      <li>Dr. Hyeonjeong Jeong (Tohoku University)</li>
      <li>Dr. Edith Kaan (University of Florida)</li>
      <li>Dr. Manuel Pulido (Pennsylvania State University)</li>
      <li>Dr. Eleanora Rossi (University of Florida)</li>
      <li>Dr. Ana Zappa (Early Career Keynote; University of Barcelona)</li>
    </ul>
  Speakers will present studies that identify, innovate, or employ methods that will help increase the ecological validity of research addressing adult second/additional language learning and processing. Based on their abstract, speakers may be invited to submit their work to a special issue in <i>Language Learning’s</i> Cognitive Neuroscience Series that will be published towards the end of 2028.
  For questions, please contact the organizers and Co-Editors of the Cognitive Neuroscience Series of <i>Language Learning</i>: Dr. Janet van Hell (<a href="mailto:jgv3@psu.edu">jgv3@psu.edu</a>; Pennsylvania State University) and Dr. Kara Morgan-Short (<a href="mailto:karams@uic.edu">karams@uic.edu</a>; University of Illinois Chicago)
